# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in contributing to this project!

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork** this repository
2. **Clone** your fork locally
3. Create a new **branch** for your feature or fix
4. **Commit** your changes with a clear message
5. **Push** to your fork
6. Open a **Pull Request** against the `main` branch

## Guidelines

- Keep changes focused and minimal
- Test your wiring changes before submitting
- Use clear and descriptive commit messages
- Be respectful in discussions and reviews

## Reporting Issues

If you find a bug or have a suggestion, open an [Issue](../../issues) with:

- A clear description of the problem or idea
- Steps to reproduce (if applicable)
- Photos of your build (if hardware related)

## What You Can Contribute

- Wiring corrections or improvements
- New module integrations
- Documentation improvements
- Translations
